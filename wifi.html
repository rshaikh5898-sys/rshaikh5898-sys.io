Change the WiFi network your ESP32 device connects to. The new credentials will be sent to the device automatically.

⚡ How this works
  1. Enter your new WiFi name and password above
  2. Click Send to ESP32 Device
  3. The credentials are saved on the server
  4. Next time the ESP32 checks in (every 30 seconds), it gets the new WiFi details
  5. ESP32 automatically disconnects and reconnects to the new WiFi ✅
